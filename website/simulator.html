What this is. A simulator of a candidate algorithm, not of PUBG Mobile. It draws zones from the rule you pick in the selector, so the battery below recovers that rule — which tests the instrument, not the game. The exported CSV matches the annotation schema in research/bluezone/schema.py, so you can push a run through the Python pipeline and check the two agree. Timings, damage and travel speed are placeholders you set yourself; the radius ratio is the one that matters most and is the first thing worth measuring from real matches.

Independent analysis toolkit. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to KRAFTON, Tencent or PUBG Mobile. Zone models test a candidate algorithm from a published patent; weapon figures are community estimates, graded by confidence.
