Three findings

0.5

Above this shrink ratio, the centre is free

A disc of radius 2r′ − r around the current centre cannot be caught outside the next zone. It is geometry, not a forecast, and it needs no match data — only the radius schedule.

See it on the chart
±0.79

Late circles can't be read from broadcast video

Circle-fitting error, carried through the key statistic, grows from ±0.012 at phase 1 to ±0.79 at phase 8, on a scale of 0 to 1. Phases 1–4 hold the usable signal.

Read the precision analysis
4 of 18

One magazine rarely covers a 1v4

Of eighteen accuracy-and-armour cases, four fit in an extended M416 magazine — none of them against Level 3 armour, which is what the survivors wear when clutches happen.

What this is, and isn't

A candidate, tested — not a leak

The zone models come from a published Tencent patent describing a centre-selection routine. A patent describes an embodiment, not shipped code, and no tournament matches have been annotated yet.

Community numbers, graded

PUBG Mobile publishes no weapon statistics. Every figure here is a private-lobby estimate, the sources disagree, and each row says how much to trust it.

Built to take your measurements

Everything computes from inputs you can replace with your own timings and tests. Quote the method, not the numbers — then measure and re-run.
