What this is. A forward simulation of a published candidate algorithm — the centre-selection routine described in Tencent's region-adjustment patent family — not a model fitted to observed matches. The radius ratio is a placeholder and propagates into every number here; measure it from your own annotated matches before quoting any of these figures. The excluded-edge band is a crude stand-in for the real terrain whitelist, whose shape is map-specific and unknown. Region area is shown next to every probability on purpose: 90% of the mass spread across 60% of the map is not a prediction.

Independent analysis toolkit. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to KRAFTON, Tencent or PUBG Mobile. Zone models test a candidate algorithm from a published patent; weapon figures are community estimates, graded by confidence.
