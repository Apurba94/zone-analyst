Margin is usable rounds minus rounds needed. A marksman rifle or sniper contributes nothing at fifteen metres, so those pairings score exactly as one gun would — which is the case for changing weapons when your last teammate goes down.

Weapon under test

Shotguns are left out: their damage figure is total pellet damage at point blank, which this model would read as a single perfect hit.

Fight assumptions

Armour reduction and swap time are unverified placeholders. Measure swap time first: the double-gun case scales with it, and nobody publishes it. Take your hit rate from your own replays, not from hope.

Weapon database

Read before quoting. PUBG Mobile publishes no weapon statistics — the game shows a power bar. Every figure here is a community private-lobby estimate, sources disagree (two put the M416 at 41 and 43), and reload times could not be sourced for most weapons. Each row carries its own confidence grade; a dash is a genuine gap, not a zero.
